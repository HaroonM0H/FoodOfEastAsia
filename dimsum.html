Pork & Shrimp Siu Mai (燒賣)

Prep Time: 45 minutes

Cook Time: 15 minutes

Total Time: 1 hour

Servings: 24 pieces (6-8 servings)

Equipment Needed:

  • Bamboo steamer or metal steamer
  • Food processor
  • Large mixing bowl
  • Wok or large pot for steaming
  • Parchment paper or cabbage leaves

Ingredients:

  • 450g ground pork (80% lean)
  • 225g raw shrimp, peeled and deveined
  • 24 round wonton wrappers
  • 100g water chestnuts, finely chopped
  • 3 green onions, finely chopped
  • 2 tablespoons soy sauce
  • 1 tablespoon sesame oil
  • 1 tablespoon Shaoxing wine
  • 1 teaspoon white pepper
  • 1 teaspoon ginger, grated
  • 1 teaspoon sugar
  • Optional garnish: carrot, peas, or tobiko

Instructions:

  1. Finely chop the shrimp into small pieces, or pulse briefly in a food processor.
  2. In a large bowl, combine ground pork, chopped shrimp, water chestnuts, and green onions.
  3. Add soy sauce, sesame oil, Shaoxing wine, white pepper, ginger, and sugar. Mix well until everything is evenly combined.
  4. Place a wonton wrapper in your palm. Add about 1.5 tablespoons of filling in the center.
  5. Gently squeeze the wrapper around the filling, forming a cylinder with the filling visible on top.
  6. Optional: Top each dumpling with your chosen garnish.
  7. Line your steamer with parchment paper or cabbage leaves.
  8. Place dumplings in the steamer, leaving space between each.
  9. Steam over high heat for 8-10 minutes until the filling is cooked through.

Tips:

  • Keep unused wrappers covered with a damp cloth to prevent drying.
  • Don't overfill the wrappers - they should remain open at the top.
  • Serve hot with soy sauce, chili oil, or Chinese black vinegar.
