Korean BBQ Bulgogi (불고기)

Prep Time: 1 hour (plus 4 hours marinating)

Cook Time: 15-20 minutes

Total Time: 5-6 hours

Servings: 4-6 people

Equipment Needed:

  • Korean BBQ grill or large skillet
  • Sharp knife for slicing meat
  • Large mixing bowl
  • Small bowls for banchan
  • Tongs for grilling
  • Food processor (for marinade)

For the Bulgogi:

  • 1kg beef sirloin or tenderloin, thinly sliced
  • 1 Asian pear, peeled and grated
  • 1 onion, grated
  • 6 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 thumb ginger, grated
  • 1/2 cup soy sauce
  • 3 tablespoons brown sugar
  • 2 tablespoons sesame oil
  • 1 tablespoon mirin
  • 1/2 teaspoon black pepper

For Serving (Banchan):

  • Steamed white rice
  • Kimchi
  • Lettuce leaves for wrapping
  • Green onions, sliced
  • Toasted sesame seeds
  • Ssamjang (dipping sauce)
  • Pickled radish
  • Garlic cloves, peeled

Instructions:

  1. Slice beef against the grain into very thin slices (partially freeze meat for easier slicing).
  2. Combine Asian pear, onion, garlic, ginger, soy sauce, brown sugar, sesame oil, mirin, and pepper in a food processor.
  3. Mix marinade with beef slices in a large bowl, ensuring even coating.
  4. Cover and refrigerate for 4 hours or overnight.
  5. Prepare banchan sides and arrange on serving plates.
  6. Heat Korean BBQ grill or skillet over medium-high heat.
  7. Cook marinated beef in batches, 2-3 minutes per side until caramelized.
  8. Garnish with sliced green onions and sesame seeds.
  9. Serve immediately with rice, lettuce leaves, and banchan.
  10. To eat, wrap meat in lettuce with rice and desired banchan.

Tips:

  • Asian pear helps tenderize the meat - you can substitute with grated apple if needed.
  • Don't overcrowd the grill/pan to ensure proper caramelization.
  • Prepare banchan in advance to focus on grilling.
  • Serve with both ssamjang and gochujang for authentic Korean flavors.
