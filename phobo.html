Pho Bo

Vietnamese Beef Pho (Phở Bò)

Prep Time: 30 minutes

Cook Time: 6-8 hours

Total Time: 7-8.5 hours

Servings: 6-8 bowls

Equipment Needed:

  • Large stockpot (at least 12 quarts)
  • Fine-mesh strainer
  • Cheesecloth
  • Large bowls for serving
  • Sharp knife for slicing beef

For the Broth:

  • 2.5 kg beef bones (mix of knuckle, marrow, and oxtail)
  • 1 whole unpeeled ginger root, halved
  • 2 whole unpeeled onions
  • 5 star anise pods
  • 6 whole cloves
  • 1 cinnamon stick
  • 1 tablespoon coriander seeds
  • 1 tablespoon fennel seeds
  • 2 tablespoons fish sauce
  • 2 tablespoons rock sugar
  • Salt to taste

For Serving:

  • 500g rice noodles (bánh phở)
  • 400g beef sirloin, thinly sliced
  • 2 cups bean sprouts
  • 1 bunch Thai basil
  • 1 bunch cilantro
  • 2 limes, cut into wedges
  • 2-3 chili peppers, sliced
  • Hoisin sauce and sriracha for serving

Instructions:

  1. Parboil the bones: Cover bones with cold water, bring to boil for 5 minutes. Drain and rinse thoroughly.
  2. Char the aromatics: Char ginger and onions under broiler or over open flame until blackened.
  3. Toast spices: In a dry pan, toast star anise, cloves, cinnamon, coriander, and fennel until fragrant.
  4. Make broth: Place cleaned bones, charred aromatics, and toasted spices in stockpot. Add water to cover by 2 inches.
  5. Simmer: Bring to boil, then reduce heat. Simmer 6-8 hours, skimming regularly.
  6. Season: Add fish sauce, rock sugar, and salt in the last hour.
  7. Strain: Remove solids and strain broth through cheesecloth.
  8. Prepare noodles: Cook rice noodles according to package instructions.
  9. Assemble: Place noodles in bowls, top with raw beef slices, and pour hot broth over.
  10. Serve: Garnish with herbs, sprouts, lime, and condiments.

Tips:

  • Freeze beef for 30 minutes before slicing to get ultra-thin slices.
  • The broth can be made ahead and frozen for up to 3 months.
  • For best results, simmer broth on the lowest possible heat.
  • Let diners add their own garnishes and sauces to taste.
