Our Story

Founded in 2020, Food of East Asia started as a passion project to bring diverse culinary traditions to food enthusiasts worldwide. Our virtual kitchen celebrates the rich tastes of East Asian cuisine, making authentic recipes accessible to home cooks everywhere.

Our Cuisines

We specialize in three distinct East Asian culinary areas:

  • Chinese: Authentic dim sum, noodles, and wok-fried specialties
  • Vietnamese: Fresh phở, bánh mì, and traditional street food
  • Korean: Flavorful bulgogi, bibimbap, and street food favorites
