Our Story

Founded in 2020, Food of East Asia started as a passion project to bring diverse culinary traditions to food enthusiasts worldwide. Our virtual kitchen celebrates the rich tastes of East Asian food, making authentic recipes accessible to home cooks everywhere.

Our Cuisines

We specialize in three distinct culinary traditions:

  • Mexican: Authentic tacos, enchiladas, and street food favorites
  • Indian: Rich curries, aromatic biryanis, and traditional tandoor dishes
  • Italian: Wood-fired pizzas, homemade pasta, and classic antipasti
